# Introduction to PVT Correlations

## What is PVT?

**PVT** stands for **Pressure-Volume-Temperature**. It describes the relationships between these three fundamental properties of hydrocarbon systems. Understanding PVT behavior is crucial for:

- Oil and gas reservoir management
- Pipeline design and operations
- Production optimization
- Economic feasibility studies

## Key Concepts

### 1. Phase Behavior

The phase behavior of hydrocarbons depends on:

- Temperature
- Pressure
- Composition

### 2. Critical Point

At the **critical point**, the liquid-gas interface disappears. This is an important thermodynamic boundary.

### 3. Saturation Pressure

The **saturation pressure** (or bubble point pressure) is where:

- Oil becomes saturated with gas
- First bubble of gas appears in oil

## Common PVT Correlations

**Standing's Correlation** - widely used for bubble point pressure
**Vasquez-Beggs Correlation** - improved accuracy for various crude types

## Applications

- Reservoir simulation
- Production forecasting
- Pipeline hydraulics
- Multiphase flow calculations
